 + How does rounding happen in IEEE 754?
   + round to even
     + "breadcrumbs" are the real numbers that can be represented exactly in IEEE 754
     + to avoid accumulating rounding error, if you're exactly between two breadcrumbs the hardware will round to the even one
       + this should round up as often as it rounds down (in principle)
