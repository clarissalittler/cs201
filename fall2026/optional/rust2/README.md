## Preparation and pacing

Topic: Optional rust2 clinic

### Optional second Rust clinic: a value with alternatives
Only after the first Rust clinic in [rust1](../rust1/Agenda.org); the required lecture 20 is about kernels.
- 10 min: retrieve move/borrow and pattern matching.
- 20 min: [structs1.rs](<structs1.rs>) and one impl method.
- 25 min: [adts1.rs](<adts1.rs>); students explain one payload-bearing variant.
- 10 min: break.
- 25 min: [fileLines.rs](<fileLines.rs>) versus [fileLines2.rs](<fileLines2.rs>), tracing Result and ?.
- 15 min: student modification to one match arm and its error path.
- 5 min: explain why an enum with payloads is more than an integer tag.
- 10 min: questions.
The enum conversion catalog and recursive lists are further reading.
[BadList.c](<BadList.c>) is intentionally uncompilable; do not include it
in a “all examples must compile” sweep. Haskell comparisons are optional.
